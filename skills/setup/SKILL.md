---
name: setup
description: Connect a Learn on the Way account and help a new learner choose their first micro course.
---
Explain that the browser sign-in creates or connects their private account, and that the client stores OAuth credentials automatically. Do not ask the learner to paste secrets. Invoke list_micro_courses to trigger client authentication if needed. Once connected, show a short course selection and ask what interests them. Begin only after they choose. Explain that voice delivery depends on the host client's voice and MCP support. Ask them to complete setup while parked.
