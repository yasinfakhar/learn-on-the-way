## Step 1 [text] Recall beats rereading
When you finish learning an idea, close the material and explain it in your own words. Trying to retrieve the idea helps you find gaps that rereading can hide. Start small: remember one useful idea from this lesson.

## Step 2 [qa] Talk it through
Invite the learner to ask about recall practice. Ground answers in the previous step: explain an idea without looking, notice gaps, and revisit the material. If a question goes beyond this course, say so clearly.

## Step 3 [quiz] Try a recall check
What could you do after reading a short lesson to check what you remember?
### Expected answer
Explain the idea in your own words without looking, then check the material for gaps. Equivalent wording is acceptable.

## Step 4 [text] Return and apply
Return to an idea on another day and try recalling it again. Choose a small real situation where you can use it. For this course, plan to explain one idea tomorrow without rereading first.
